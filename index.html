About Romex Establishment

Established in 1987 in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India, Romex Establishment is a leading manufacturer, exporter, supplier, dealer, and distributor of plastic injection mouldings, premium bath fittings, and hardware items.

Under the brand name Romex, we provide supreme quality PTMT, ABS, and PVC products at economical costs. All items are manufactured using eco-friendly methods and backed by state-of-the-art technologies, robust systems, and skilled personnel to ensure a best-in-class quality management system.
